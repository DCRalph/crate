Wallbox

Crate as the selector panel of the house jukebox. A cabinet that is petrol green by night and seafoam enamel by day, ivory printed strips with a coral band, amber lamps for what is live, and keys you can feel go down.

The idea

A jukebox is a shared machine with one printed list everybody reads and one window that says what is on. That is what a home media server is, so the interface borrows its parts.

  • Titles are stripsName on ivory, artist on the coral band. The same strip labels an album, the track playing, and a film.
  • Printed matter is ivoryLists, tables and forms are cards set into the cabinet. The cabinet itself holds only art, tabs and the deck.
  • Lamps mean liveAmber is lit only for what is playing or switched on. A hollow ring is off. Nothing else is amber.
  • Every row has a numberType it to play. The selection window rolls to the new number once.
  • Night and dayThe cabinet is petrol by night, seafoam by day. Print is ivory in both, and the deck is always night.

Shell

No sidebar. The library is a row of tabs standing on an ivory hinge line, the way a wallbox turns its pages. Admin is one tab that opens a second row; your own settings live under your initial. Only the cabinet changes between night and day. The deck along the bottom is always night, so the lamp and its number read the same at any hour.

Motion

Everything plays once and stops. Keys go down 1px when pressed. The selection number rolls in when a track starts. A page turns with one short slide. Nothing loops, and all of it yields to reduced motion.

In code

Tokens live in globals.css under the shadcn names, so components need no colour of their own. The root holds the cabinet (background, foreground, muted, border) for night under .dark and day without it. Anything printed (cards, tables, menus, dialogs, sheets) carries .print, which re-declares the same names in ivory and ink, so a muted label is right on either ground. .night does the same for the deck. Capitals come from CSS, never from stored text.

Honest risk

It is a themed look, and themes can tire. Ivory cards are bright for a dark room, which is why browsing pages keep covers on the cabinet and save print for lists and forms. On a phone the tab row scrolls sideways.

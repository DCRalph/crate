Crate style guide

Self-hosted music server. Web app and admin console. Dark only. "Crate" is a working name and lives in one constant.

This is your WMGX console system carried over: same shadcn tokens, same type roles, same shell. The changes are true black behind content, a player bar, and patterns for music lists.

Principles

  • The music is the colour. The interface stays neutral grey on black. Album art is the only saturated thing on most screens.
  • Red means one thing at a time. Brand red marks the current page, the one primary action on a screen, and what is playing or liked. Nothing else.
  • Dense, not cramped. 14px body, 36px controls, hairline rows. Show the table, not a summary of the table.
  • No decoration. Cards only where a group of settings needs a boundary. Lists and tables sit straight on the page.
  • Everything plays once. Motion answers something that just happened, then stops. Nothing loops, so an idle page costs nothing.
  • Dry, a little knowing. The voice is someone flipping through a crate of records. One line of character where a screen is empty or something went wrong. Never in the middle of a task.

Shape and spacing

  • Radius: 8px controls, 10 to 14px cards and dialogs, 6px album art, full for avatars and status dots.
  • Controls are 36px tall, 32px in toolbars and table rows. Icon buttons are square at the same heights.
  • Page padding 16 / 24 / 32px by breakpoint. Sections are 32px apart, a heading sits 12px above its content.
  • Hairlines are 1px at white 10%. Rows divide with a hairline, not zebra stripes or gaps.
  • Icons are Lucide, 16px, 2px stroke, in the text colour.

Shell

Sidebar on the sidebar grey, content on black with a rounded top-left corner, sticky header that carries the page title as a breadcrumb, player bar across the bottom. The sidebar collapses to icons on desktop and becomes a sheet on phones. Admin group only shows for admins.

Track list

The core pattern. A plain table straight on the page. The playing row turns its title brand-text red and swaps its number for level bars. Double click or the row's play button starts it; on the playing row the same button pauses. A liked track keeps its heart showing; an empty heart waits for hover. Row actions sit in a menu on the right, visible on hover and always visible on touch.

Em dashes, exclamation marks, emoji

  • Sentence case everywhere except t-label and the wordmark.
  • Say what happened, with the number. Errors say what to do next.
  • Destructive confirms name the thing: "Revoke Symfonium? It will stop working straight away."

Motion and feedback

The record is the one authored idea: when a track starts, the record in the mark gets a single turn. Everything else is feedback or arrival, and all of it ends.

  • One easing for arrivals: --ease-out-expo, a quick start and a long soft landing. No bounce, no elastic.
  • No pulse, shimmer, spinner or anything on infinite. Waiting is shown as a number and a real progress bar ("1,204 of 8,310").
  • Reduced motion keeps colour and fades and drops everything that moves. It is one rule in globals.css, so new motion gets it for free.
  • Results of an action are a Sonner toast, bottom right, above the player bar.
  • Focus ring is 3px at ring grey 50%, on every interactive element.
  • Text selection is brand red, the caret is brand-text, scrollbars are thin and grey.

In code

  • Tokens in src/app/globals.css (shadcn variables plus --brand and --brand-text). Utilities t-label, t-display, font-title.
  • shadcn components in src/components/ui, unmodified apart from the brand button variant.
  • Fonts through next/font: Montserrat, Cabin, Anybody (width axis).
  • Dark only. class="dark" is fixed on html; there is no light theme to maintain.
